The account menu at the bottom of the Compute sidebar gives you access to personal settings, organization management, appearance, language, and sign-out controls.

## Open the account menu

Select the menu next to your account at the bottom of the left sidebar. Depending on your account and role, it contains:

- **Settings**
- **Organization**
- **Theme**
- **Language**
- **Sign out**

Personal settings apply to your own Compute account. Organization actions and billing permissions depend on whether you are an Owner, Admin, or Member.

## Organization

Select **Organization** to open the organization page. You can also select the organization name in the upper-left corner, then choose **Organization options**.

The organization page shows its Owner, Admins, Members, and pending invitations. The actions available to you depend on your role.

- **Owners** can manage people, billing, ownership, and organization deletion.
- **Admins** can manage people and organization settings and view billing details.
- **Members** can view the people in the organization and use its resources, but cannot manage the organization.

See [How organizations work in Compute](/documentation/organizations/how-organizations-work) for the full role comparison.

## Theme

Choose how the Compute console looks:

- **System:** Matches the theme used by your operating system or browser.
- **Light:** Uses a light background and dark text.
- **Dark:** Uses a dark background and light text.

This changes only the appearance of the Compute console for your account.

## Language

Use **Language** to change the language of the Compute interface.

The available languages currently include:

- English
- French

Changing the interface language does not affect your organization, resources, or technical configuration.

## SSH key

Your SSH public key is used to authorize access to new instances you create.

To replace it:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Find the SSH key field.
3. Paste the new public key.
4. Select **Modify** to save it.

The new key applies to instances you create afterward.

<Warning>
  Replacing the SSH key in Settings does not update instances that already exist. Add or remove keys manually on those instances when needed.
</Warning>

For operating-system-specific instructions, see:

- [Add an SSH key on Linux](/documentation/connectivity/ssh-key-linux)
- [Add an SSH key on macOS](/documentation/connectivity/ssh-key-macos)
- [Add an SSH key on Windows](/documentation/connectivity/ssh-key-windows)

## Delete account

Select **Request to delete account** only when you are ready to permanently remove your personal Compute account.

Before deletion, you must resolve anything that still ties the account to active Compute resources:

- Terminate running instances.
- Leave your current organization.
- Transfer ownership or delete the organization if you are its Owner.

<Danger>
  Account deletion is irreversible. Download important data before terminating instances or deleting an organization.
</Danger>

Follow [Delete your Compute account](/documentation/account-billing/delete-account) for the complete preparation and deletion process.
